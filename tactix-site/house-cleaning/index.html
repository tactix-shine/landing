A clean that starts with your list.

Some people want the whole place done. Others want the kitchen and bathrooms sorted and the rest left alone. Tell us what matters to you and we'll quote for that clean, not a standard package.

People usually get in touch when they're:

  • Getting the house ready before guests arrive
  • Catching up after a busy stretch at work or a new baby
  • After a deeper clean of rooms that don't get regular attention

What to tell us for a quote.

  • Your suburb
  • The type of home and the number of bedrooms and bathrooms
  • The rooms you want cleaned
  • Anything that needs extra attention, such as the oven, shower screens or skirting boards
  • Pets, parking or access instructions
  • The days and times that suit you

We confirm what's covered before the clean goes ahead.

House cleaning questions.

Can I ask for specific rooms only?

Yes. Tell us which rooms you want done and we'll quote for those.

How is the price worked out?

We quote based on the size of your home, its condition and the rooms you want cleaned. You get the quote before the clean goes ahead.

Which parts of Melbourne do you cover?

We clean homes across Melbourne. Tell us your suburb when you get in touch and we'll confirm we can get to you.
