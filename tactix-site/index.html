Common questions.

Do you clean for NDIS participants?

Yes. Tell us what you need help with. If you're using NDIS funding, check with your plan manager or support coordinator how cleaning fits in your plan. More about NDIS cleaning.

Which parts of Melbourne do you clean?

We clean homes across Melbourne. Tell us your suburb when you get in touch and we'll confirm we can get to you.

How much does a clean cost?

It depends on the size of the home, its condition and what you want done. We quote once you've told us about your place, so the price matches the job.

Are your cleaners background checked?

Yes. Every cleaner holds NDIS Worker Screening clearance, a police check and a Working with Children Check.
